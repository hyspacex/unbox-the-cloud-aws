## G1 · Lawful basis stated
The folder names the lawful basis for processing personal data (GDPR Art. 6).
<!-- keel:offline {"kind":"require_all","clauses":[{"label":"lawful basis","any":["lawful basis[^.\\n]{0,80}(consent|contract|legal obligation|vital interest|public task|legitimate interest)"]}]} -->

## G2 · Personal data categories listed
The folder lists which categories of personal data are processed (GDPR Art. 30).
<!-- keel:offline {"kind":"require_all","clauses":[{"label":"categories of personal data","any":["process\\w*[^.\\n]{0,40}(customer names|names|email addresses|account numbers|phone numbers)"]}]} -->

## G3 · Data subject requests handled
The folder explains how access requests and deletion requests from data subjects are handled (GDPR Art. 15 and 17).
<!-- keel:offline {"kind":"require_all","clauses":[{"label":"deletion requests","any":["deletion requests?","erasure requests?","right to erasure"]},{"label":"access requests","any":["access requests?","subject access","right of access"]}]} -->

## G4 · International transfers addressed
Either personal data stays in the EU, or the transfer safeguard is named (for example Standard Contractual Clauses).
<!-- keel:offline {"kind":"require_all","clauses":[{"label":"EU-only processing or transfer safeguard","any":["never leave the EU","stays? in the EU","standard contractual clauses","\\bSCCs?\\b","adequacy decision"]}]} -->

## G5 · DPIA referenced
A data protection impact assessment is completed or referenced (GDPR Art. 35).
<!-- keel:offline {"kind":"require_all","clauses":[{"label":"DPIA","any":["\\bDPIA\\b","data protection impact assessment"]}]} -->
