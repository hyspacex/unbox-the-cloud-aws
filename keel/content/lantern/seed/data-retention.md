# Lantern — Data retention

Author: J. Park · Approved by Privacy (Priya Natarajan) on 2026-10-15

## Conversation data

Conversation text sent to the Suggestion API is retained for 30 days to support quality review, then deleted.
Suggestions that agents accept are stored with the case record under the bank's existing case-retention schedule.

## Logs

Application logs are retained for 90 days. Security events in the SIEM follow the SIEM's 1-year policy.

## Data subject requests

Customer deletion requests remove conversation text within 30 days through the case-management deletion workflow.
Customer access requests are answered within 30 days through the bank's privacy portal.
